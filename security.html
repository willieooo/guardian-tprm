Security & Trust

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Guardian TPRM exists to help you document and evidence your own third-party risk programme. It only makes sense that we hold ourselves to the same standard. Here's an honest account of how the platform is built and protected — not a marketing checklist.

A note on certifications: Guardian TPRM's founding team has deep, practising expertise across ISO 27001, ISO 22301, SOC 2, and related frameworks — it's the same expertise the platform is built to help you apply to your own suppliers. We have not yet completed an independent third-party audit or certification of the Guardian TPRM platform itself, and we won't claim otherwise. If and when that changes, this page will say so, with the certificate to back it up.

Data isolation

Every organisation's data is isolated at the database level using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) — not just application-level checks. Every query the platform runs is scoped to the requesting organisation by the database itself, so one client organisation cannot see another's suppliers, questionnaires, or risk data, regardless of what the application layer does.

Access control

Role-based permissions

Admin, Sub-Admin, and standard user roles, each with a distinct scope of what they can see and change.

No account needed for suppliers

Suppliers complete questionnaires via a unique, token-authenticated link — no password to create, lose, or reuse elsewhere.

Time-boxed support access

If our platform support team ever needs to view your organisation's data to help you, access is read-only, automatically expires, and is fully logged — never open-ended.

Immutable audit trail

Every meaningful action in the platform — supplier changes, questionnaire dispatch, corrective actions — is logged with a timestamp and the user responsible.

Encryption

All traffic to and from Guardian TPRM — the marketing site, the application, and the supplier portal — is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. Data at rest is encrypted by our infrastructure provider, Supabase, using industry-standard encryption.

Payment data

We never see or store your full card details. All payment processing is handled directly by Stripe, a PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor used by millions of businesses globally. Guardian TPRM only ever receives confirmation that a payment succeeded.
