Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

In plain terms: Guardian TPRM is a third-party risk management platform. When your organisation uses it, we store the account, billing, and supplier-risk data you and your team enter. We don't sell data, we don't run advertising trackers, and this site currently uses no analytics or tracking cookies at all.

1. Who we are

Guardian TPRM ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Bloumbherg Consultants. This policy covers both the marketing website at guardian-tprm.com and the platform at app.guardian-tprm.com.

A note on supplier data specifically

When your organisation enters information about a third-party supplier — including a supplier contact's name and email, so we can dispatch a questionnaire — your organisation is the data controller for that information, and Guardian TPRM acts as a data processor on your behalf. If you are a supplier who received a questionnaire link, the organisation that sent it to you is responsible for how your response data is used; Guardian TPRM processes it on their instructions.

3. Cookies and tracking

The guardian-tprm.com marketing website does not currently use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics. The application at app.guardian-tprm.com uses only the essential session data required to keep you signed in.

5. Where data is processed and stored

Guardian TPRM serves organisations across the United Kingdom, Nigeria, South Africa, and Kenya. Depending on your organisation's location, one or more of the following may apply to how your data is protected:

  • United Kingdom / EU: UK GDPR and, where relevant, EU GDPR
  • Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)
  • South Africa: Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
  • Kenya: Data Protection Act, 2019

Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Vercel) may process and store data in regions outside your own country. Where this involves a cross-border transfer of personal data, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect it.

6. How long we keep data

We retain account and supplier-risk data for as long as your organisation's account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to meet audit, legal, and contractual obligations typical of a compliance record-keeping platform. If you'd like your organisation's data deleted sooner, contact us using the details below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us — see Section 9.

8. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect data, including encryption in transit, role-based access control, and audit logging of platform activity. No system is completely immune to risk, and we continually work to improve our safeguards.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your data, can be sent to: privacy@guardian-tprm.com

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
